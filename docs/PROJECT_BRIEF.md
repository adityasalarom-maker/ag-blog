# Project Brief — Creator-Led Literary & Visual-Art Publication

**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Project:** AG Literary & Visual Art Publication  
**Owner / Editor:** Single creator/editor  
**Target Surface:** Google Antigravity IDE  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This publication is a creator-led, bilingual (Hindi & English) digital magazine and gallery dedicated to poetry, narrative writing, essays, personal reflections, philosophical thoughts, curated quotations, and visual artworks (illustrations, digital art, visual poetry, and creative crafts).

It provides an editorial, magazine-style experience on the front page, calm typography-led reading experiences for literary works, and an interactive, accessible gallery for visual arts.

---

## 2. Core Identity and Positioning

- **Editorial Brand:** A distinctive, recognizable literary masthead with attribution to its creator/editor.
- **Not a standard blog:** Distinct content taxonomy (poems, long-form stories, essays, thoughts vs. sourced quotations, artwork portfolios, collections).
- **Not an open social platform:** No public registration, reader comments, likes, or user-submitted content at launch.
- **Bilingual Nature:** Hindi (in Devanagari and Romanized script) and English. Flexible relationships between independent works and translated language editions.

---

## 3. Target Audience & Reader Experience

- **Audience:** Readers, writers, art enthusiasts, and thinkers seeking high-quality bilingual literature and art.
- **Reading Comfort:** Distraction-free reading view, reader-selectable themes (Light, Dark, Sepia), font-size scaling, responsive layouts, and strict poetry line/stanza preservation.
- **Sharing & Discovery:** Canonical share URLs, WhatsApp sharing, Web Share API with clipboard fallback, structured metadata for rich social previews.

---

## 4. Editorial & Content Scope

1. **Poetry:** Preserved line breaks, stanza spacing, meter rhythm, script markers (Devanagari, Romanized, English).
2. **Stories & Long-form Writing:** Multi-paragraph fiction, memoirs, essays, and articles with reading time and progress indicators.
3. **Thoughts vs. Quotations:** Clear visual and structural distinction between original aphorisms/reflections and attributed source quotations.
4. **Visual Art & Craft Gallery:** High-resolution responsive art display with accessible lightbox zoom and metadata (medium, year, dimensions, rights).
5. **Collections:** Curated anthologies grouping poems, essays, and art across types without duplicate records.
6. **Bilingual Editions:** Linked translations preserving reciprocal edition links.
7. **Creator & Static Pages:** About the creator, publication manifesto, contact details, copyright & rights policy, and privacy notice.

---

## 5. Budget, Infrastructure & Free-Tier Guardrails

- **Zero-to-low initial hosting cost:** Leverage robust free tiers (Cloudflare Pages for edge hosting and static delivery, future headless CMS free tier).
- **No lock-in:** Plain markdown/JSON exportability for all content records and media manifests.
- **Privacy & Rights:** Public content is strictly decoupled from private internal notes, drafts, and copyright evidence. No confidential documents on public CDNs.
- **Explicit Approvals:** No account creation, domain purchase, paid provisioning, or deployment without explicit owner sign-off.

---

## 6. Explicit Non-Goals (Launch Phase)

- No public user accounts, reader login, or member paywalls.
- No public comment sections or threaded discussion boards.
- No social networking features (no upvotes, claps, follower counts).
- No automated machine translation; all translations are editorially curated.
- No legal certification or copyright registration claims; declarations are marked as creator-declared.
